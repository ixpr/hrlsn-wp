international Delivery

As soon as your order is dispatched you will receive a shipping confirmation with a tracking link. The link is active when the carrier has picked up and scanned your shipment, which is why the link may not work the minute you receive it in the shipping confirmation. Please use the tracking number provided to track your order and see the scheduled day of delivery. The courier won't be able to contact you via phone and will attempt to deliver your parcel three times. If unsuccessful, you will need to contact DHL and pick up the parcel yourself.

HOW LONG DOES SHIPPING TAKE?

Standard shipping (EU, UK): 2-6 business days

Express shipping (EU, UK, CIS*, other European locations): 1-3 business days

Express shipping (US, UAE, Australia, Canada, Middle East): 2-4 business days

Please note that the delivery dates are approximate and we cannot promise a specific date for delivery. Please refer to shipping timeframes at the checkout for more information on your destination. We are unable to warn you in advance as to whether a delivery may be delayed due to customs or busy periods. We are trying our very best to get your order to you as soon as possible.

returns

We hope you are satisfied with your purchase, but if necessary, your order can be returned in whole or in part within 14 days of receipt. Do not use the product you plan to return - it must remain new. Save all tags and labels. To return your purchase, please contact us by email at kk@hrlsn.co, quoting your order number, for further instructions.

Shipping costs for returns are borne by the buyer. Once your return has been received and processed, you will be sent a confirmation email.
